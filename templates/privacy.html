who else sees it

  • signed-in players see the login of each round's winner, and how many people picked each number. what you picked is never shown next to your name. visitors who are not signed in see the numbers only, no names.
  • 42 (42 intra) learns that you signed in here.
  • 42 Vienna's coalition points service gets the 42 user ids of who played each round and who won it, so 42 can award coalition points.

the site runs on our own server, with no hosting company in between. nothing is loaded from other sites: no outside fonts, scripts or images. we don't sell or share anything else, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers.

signing in with GitHub or Google

coming soon, alongside 42. when it arrives, Google will share only your email address, and GitHub your username, account age and email address; they are kept to run your account and sign you in (Art. 6(1)(b)), until you delete it, and never used for anything else. GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA) and Google (Google LLC, USA) then learn that you signed in here, when you choose them, and transfer data to the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. this page is updated with the details before it goes live.

cookies and storage

  • ig42_session: keeps you signed in. set when you sign in; strictly necessary.
  • your browser's local storage remembers that you dismissed the result of a round, so it doesn't pop up again.

nothing is set before you sign in, so there is no cookie banner. 42 sets its own cookies on its own site, not on this one.

age

you need to be 14 or older to sign in: younger than that, Austrian law (§ 4 DSG, Art. 8 GDPR) needs a parent’s consent, which we don’t collect. if we learn an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

your rights

you can see, correct, take with you or delete what we hold about you, and object to what we do on the basis of our interests. write to the contact in the impressum.

you can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority: Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Wien, dsb.gv.at.
