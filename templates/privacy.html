privacy

i guess low keeps only what it needs to run the game. this page says what that is, why, for how long, and who else sees any of it. who runs the site is in the impressum.

who else sees it

  • other players see your username next to rounds and wins, once you are signed in. signed-out visitors see a label like "player #3fa2" instead.
  • 42 (42 intra, EU), GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA) and Google (Google LLC, USA) learn that you signed in here, when you choose them. GitHub and Google transfer data to the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Resend (Resend Inc., USA) delivers the mails we send: the link to confirm your address, and password resets. it sees your address and the mail.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare Inc., USA), when Turnstile is switched on, checks the sign-up form for bots and sees your network address while it does.

we don't sell or share anything else, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers.

cookies and storage

  • ig42_session: keeps you signed in. set when you sign in; strictly necessary.
  • ig42_held: holds a number you picked before signing in, for up to an hour, so it can be locked once you do. strictly necessary; only set if you pick a number while signed out.
  • your browser's local storage remembers that you dismissed the result of a round, so it doesn't pop up again.

nothing is set before you sign in or pick a number, so there is no cookie banner. the sign-in providers set their own cookies on their own sites, not on this one.

your rights

you can see, correct, take with you or delete what we hold about you, and object to what we do on the basis of our interests. most of it is on your account page: rename, remove a login, or delete the account, which removes your username, logins, email and password straight away. for anything else, write to the contact in the impressum.

you can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority: Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Wien, dsb.gv.at.
