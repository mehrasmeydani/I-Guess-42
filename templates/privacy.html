who else sees it

  • everyone, signed in or not, sees your username next to the global rounds you won, on the leaderboards (points and wins) and in shared links. the 42 round, with your 42 login, is only shown to signed-in 42 students. what you picked is only ever shown as a count per number. your username is public; pick one you are happy to show, and change it on your account page.
  • moderators, named by the operator, see the patterns above for the global round: shared networks as hashes, when accounts sign in and guess, and what was picked in rounds that have closed. never today's guesses.
  • 42 (42 intra, EU), GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA) and Google (Google LLC, USA) learn that you signed in here, when you choose them. GitHub and Google transfer data to the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • 42 Vienna's coalition points service, for the 42 round only: the 42 user ids of who played each round and who won it, so 42 can award coalition points.
  • Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., trading as Resend, USA) delivers the mails we send: confirming your address, password resets, “you already have an account”, and a moderator’s warning. it sees your address and the mail, and stores them in the USA as our processor (Art. 28 GDPR), under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU standard contractual clauses.

the site runs on our own server, with no hosting company in between. the robot check on the sign-up form is our own: a small puzzle your browser solves, checked here. no outside service sees it. nothing is loaded from other sites: no outside fonts, scripts or images. we don't sell or share anything else, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers.

age

you need to be 14 or older to sign in or make an account: younger than that, Austrian law (§ 4 DSG, Art. 8 GDPR) needs a parent’s consent, which we don’t collect. if we learn an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

fair play

one person, one account, played by hand. accounts run by a script, or several accounts used to take numbers so another one wins, are not allowed. moderators look for the patterns above. a flagged account gets one warning by email; if it goes on, it is banned and marked, and rounds it bent can be called off or have their points taken back.

cookies and storage

  • ig42_session: keeps you signed in. set when you sign in; strictly necessary.
  • ig42_oauth_state: set when you start signing in with 42, GitHub or Google, so the sign-in only finishes in the browser that started it. gone after 15 minutes at most; strictly necessary.
  • ig42_held: holds a number you picked before signing in, for up to an hour, so it can be locked once you do. strictly necessary; only set if you pick a number while signed out.
  • your browser's local storage remembers that you dismissed the result of a round, so it doesn't pop up again.

nothing is set before you start signing in or pick a number, so there is no cookie banner. the sign-in providers set their own cookies on their own sites, not on this one.

your rights

you can see, correct, take with you or delete what we hold about you, and object to what we do on the basis of our interests. most of it is on your account page: rename, remove a login, or delete the account, which removes your username, logins, email, password, 42 login and name, network hashes and any warning or ban reason straight away. your rounds and points stay, under deleted- and a number, so past results and pots still add up. for anything else, write to the contact in the impressum.

you can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority: Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Wien, dsb.gv.at.
