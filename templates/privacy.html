what we keep, and why

i guess low keeps only what it needs to run the game.

  • your account: a username, when you joined, and the ways you sign in (your 42, GitHub or Google id, and the email they report). if you sign in with an email, that address and a hash of your password — never the password itself.
  • 42 students: your 42 login, display name and campus, from intra, refreshed when you sign in.
  • your guesses, one per round, and when you made them. they're secret until the round closes, then public.
  • where you came from, only as a keyed hash that changes every month, so moderators can see that several accounts share a network, and nothing about where it is. not your address itself.
  • one cookie, to keep you signed in. no tracking, no ads, no analytics.

who else sees it

  • other players see usernames next to rounds and wins. in the 42 Vienna round, logins are only shown to other 42 students.
  • sign-in providers (42, GitHub, Google) learn that you signed in here, when you use them.
  • Resend delivers the few emails we send: confirming your address and resetting a password.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, if it is switched on, checks the sign-up form for bots.

how long, and how to leave

your account stays until you delete it, from your account page. that removes your username, logins, email and password straight away. the rounds you played stay, under deleted- and a number, so their results still add up. sign-in sessions end after 30 days; emailed links after a day.

to see what we hold about you, or for anything else, see the imprint.
